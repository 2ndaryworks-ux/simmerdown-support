Privacy Policy

Simmer Down: Noise Meter

SupportPrivacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026. Published by Secondary Works. This policy covers the Simmer Down app for iPhone.

Microphone

Simmer Down uses your device's microphone only to measure how loud the room is. The sound is turned into a loudness number on your device and immediately thrown away. No audio is recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and nobody, including us, can hear what is said in the room.

Information you type

Vote options, team names, the Class Jar reward and your settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them.

Advertising

Simmer Down shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads and measure how they perform, Google may collect:

  • your device's IP address, used to estimate your general location (city or region, not GPS)
  • a device identifier used for advertising
  • which ads you see and tap
  • crash and performance information

Simmer Down marks every ad request as directed to children and limits ads to general-audience content. That means ads are not personalized and the app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Learn how Google uses information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some US states, Simmer Down shows Google's consent message. You can change your choices any time in Settings > Privacy choices in the app.
  • You can turn off microphone access any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it saved on your device.

Children

Simmer Down is meant to be run by teachers and parents. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no accounts and never asks for names, email addresses or other contact details.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
