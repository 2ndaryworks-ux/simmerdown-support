The meter isn't moving

Simmer Down needs the microphone to hear the room. Go to iPhone Settings > Simmer Down and turn on Microphone. If another app is using the microphone, such as a call or a voice recording, close it and reopen Simmer Down.

It's too strict or too easy

  • Party: tap the gear icon and change Party limit. A higher level lets the room get louder before a warning.
  • Classroom quiet games: change How quiet on the game's setup screen.
  • Every mode: use the Microphone sensitivity slider in Settings. Phones hear differently, so slide toward Less if a quiet room already shows tall bars, or toward More if loud talking barely moves the meter.

There's no sound

Check the silent switch on the side of your iPhone, turn up the volume, and make sure Sound effects is on in the app's Settings.

Show it on a TV

Open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring and choose your Apple TV or AirPlay TV. The TV shows a big-screen view of the meter and games, and you keep using the phone as the remote.

Skip the opening

Tap the screen during the opening to skip it, or turn off Play the opening in Settings.

Ads and privacy choices

Simmer Down is free and shows ads from Google AdMob between games, never while a game is listening. If you live in the EU, the UK, Switzerland or certain US states, you can change your ad privacy choices any time in the app's Settings > Privacy choices. Read the full Privacy Policy.
